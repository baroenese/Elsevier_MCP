# Topik Tren Computing 2021–2026 beserta Paper Berimpak Tinggi (WoS & Scopus)

Enam klaster topik paling tren dalam 5 tahun terakhir: (1) LLM/Generative AI, (2) Edge Computing & IoT, (3) Federated Learning, (4) Graph Neural Networks, (5) Keamanan AI/adversarial ML & cybersecurity, dan (6) Digital Twins & Quantum/6G. Paper-paper di bawah semuanya terbit di jurnal Q1 yang terindeks Web of Science (SCIE, memiliki JIF dari JCR) sekaligus Scopus.

## Topik 1 — Large Language Models & Generative AI (paling eksplosif)

- **Review:** Z. Zhao et al., *"A Survey of Large Language Models"* (2023) — ±8.500+ sitasi, survei LLM dominan periode ini ([arXiv:2303.18223](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.18223), terbit juga di *Frontiers of Computer Science*).
- **Review:** Z. Ji et al., *"Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation"* — *ACM Computing Surveys* (2023), salah satu paper CSUR terbanyak disitasi ([ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3571730)).
- **Review:** *"Instruction Tuning for Large Language Models: A Survey"* — *ACM Computing Surveys* ([ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3777411)).

## Topik 2 — Edge Computing & IoT

- **Review:** *"A Comprehensive Survey on Communication-Efficient Federated Learning in Mobile Edge Environments"* — *IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials* (2025).
- Pendahulu yang menjadi referensi wajib (2020, tepat di batas window): X. Wang et al., *"Convergence of Edge Computing and Deep Learning: A Comprehensive Survey"* — *IEEE Communications Surveys & Tutorials*, ±1.985 sitasi ([IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8976180/)).

## Topik 3 — Federated Learning (kini menyatu dengan LLM)

- **Research article:** Y. Cheng et al., *"Toward Federated Large Language Models: Motivations, Methods, and Future Directions"* (2024, ±94 sitasi) ([IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10759678/)).
- **Review:** F. Piccialli et al., *"Federated and Edge Learning for Large Language Models"* — *Information Fusion* (2025) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1566253524006183)).

## Topik 4 — Graph Neural Networks

- **Review:** *"Adversarial Attack and Defense on Graph Data: A Survey"* — *IEEE TPAMI* (2022) ([IEEE Xplore](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/9878092/)).

## Topik 5 — Keamanan AI, Adversarial ML & Cybersecurity

- **Review:** H. Baniecki et al., *"Adversarial Attacks and Defenses in Explainable Artificial Intelligence"* — *Information Fusion* (2023/24, ±294 sitasi) — irisan XAI + adversarial.
- **Review:** *"Adversarial Machine Learning in Industry: A Systematic Literature Review"* — *Computers & Security* (2024) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167404824002931)).
- Sub-tren naik: post-quantum cryptography dan AI-driven threat detection.

- **Review:** T. Kreuzer et al., *"Artificial Intelligence in Digital Twins — A Systematic Literature Review"* — *Information and Software Technology* (2024, ±290 sitasi) ([ScienceDirect](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169023X24000284)).

## Catatan Metodologi

- **WoS-indexed**: semua jurnal pada tabel adalah SCIE (memiliki JIF resmi dari JCR). **Scopus-indexed**: semuanya juga terindeks Scopus dengan SJR/CiteScore Q1.
- Nilai JIF berubah tiap tahun (contoh: COMST melonjak 34.4 → 46.7 → 50.6); cek selalu JCR terbaru dan [Scimago](https://www.scimagojr.com/) sebelum submit.
- Kunci Scopus API (`ELSEVIER_API_KEY`) belum tersedia saat penelusuran; jika sudah diisi, query live `search_papers` (sort by citations, filter `PUBYEAR > 2020`) dapat memverifikasi sitasi terkini per topik.
